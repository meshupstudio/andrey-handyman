Common Questions

Service Area FAQs

Do you charge extra for travel outside Fresno?

No travel fees for any city listed on this page. Clovis, Sanger, Madera, Reedley, Selma, Kerman, and Fowler are all within our standard service area. For locations further out, we'll let you know upfront if there's any additional cost — and it's usually a small flat fee, not an ongoing charge.

Do you offer free estimates in all service areas?

Yes — free on-site estimates throughout our entire service area. Andrey personally visits every home before quoting any project, regardless of city. We respond to all estimate requests within 24 hours.

How quickly can you start a project in Clovis or Sanger?

Scheduling depends on our current workload, but we typically have availability within 2–4 weeks for new projects. We'll give you a clear start date in writing as part of your estimate — not a vague "sometime next month."

Are you familiar with local building codes outside Fresno?

Yes. We're familiar with building codes and permit requirements across Fresno County and neighboring jurisdictions. For permitted work — plumbing relocations, electrical upgrades — we handle the permit process for you in any city we serve.

My city isn't listed — can you still help?

Possibly. We regularly travel beyond our listed areas for the right project. Call us at (559) 360-4128 or fill out the estimate form with your location and we'll let you know right away.

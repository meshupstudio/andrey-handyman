How Much Does a Bathroom Remodel Cost in Fresno, CA?

2025 Guide — straight from a local contractor who's built hundreds of them.

Andrey Boginski Owner, Andrey's Construction & Remodeling Inc. January 2025 8 min read

One of the first questions every Fresno homeowner asks me: "How much is this going to cost?" It's a fair question, and I'm going to give you a straight answer — not a "it depends" non-answer that leaves you no better off than before you started reading.

I've been doing bathroom remodels in Fresno and Clovis for over 20 years. Here's what projects actually cost in 2025, broken down by project type, with notes on what drives costs up or down.

Bottom line up front: Most Fresno bathroom remodels fall between $8,000 and $25,000. Basic cosmetic refreshes start around $4,000. Full gut-and-rebuild projects with custom tile and a walk-in shower run $20,000–$35,000+.

These ranges reflect current labor and material costs in the Fresno metro as of early 2025. Prices here run somewhat lower than the Bay Area or LA but have gone up with inflation — materials especially are 20–30% higher than they were in 2020.

What Drives the Cost Up or Down?

1. Size of the Bathroom

A 5×8 hall bathroom and a 12×14 master suite are completely different projects. More square footage means more tile, more demo work, more time, and a larger material order. Square footage is one of the biggest cost drivers — not the only one, but usually the starting point for any estimate.

2. Tile Selection

Tile material and pattern choices can swing a budget by thousands of dollars. Here's a rough material cost breakdown (supply only, not labor):

  • Standard ceramic: $1–$4/sq ft
  • Porcelain: $3–$10/sq ft
  • Large-format porcelain (24×24 and up): $5–$15/sq ft
  • Natural stone (travertine, marble): $8–$25/sq ft
  • Mosaic accents: $15–$40/sq ft

Labor for setting large-format tile or stone also costs more — it requires more precision cutting and careful layout. A herringbone or offset pattern adds time too.

3. Plumbing Changes

If you're keeping the toilet, shower, and sink in the same locations, plumbing costs are minimal. Moving any fixture — especially the shower drain or toilet rough-in — adds significant cost. Expect $800–$2,500+ for meaningful plumbing relocation.

4. Structural Issues Found During Demo

This is where budgets sometimes get revised. When we open up walls, we occasionally find water damage, mold, subfloor rot, or outdated knob-and-tube wiring. In older Fresno homes (pre-1980s especially), this is not unusual. We always flag these upfront in your estimate with allowances, but it's worth budgeting 10–15% above your quote as a contingency.

5. Materials You Supply vs. We Supply

Some homeowners like to source their own tile, vanity, or fixtures to save money or get exactly the look they want. That's fine — we work with owner-supplied materials. Just be aware that if materials arrive damaged or short, it can affect your timeline. We always verify quantities before ordering.

What's Included in Our Quotes

When I quote a project, you get a line-item breakdown that includes:

  • Demo and disposal
  • Waterproofing and substrate prep
  • Tile material and setting labor
  • Fixture and vanity installation
  • Plumbing and electrical work (in-house)
  • Paint (one coat primer + two finish coats on new drywall)
  • Permit fees if required
  • Final cleanup

There are no "allowances" buried in the total that can balloon later. If something is unknown (like tile selection), we'll hold a realistic material allowance that we adjust once you've chosen your materials.

Do You Need a Permit in Fresno?

Permits are required for plumbing relocation, electrical work beyond like-for-like replacement, and structural changes. For a standard bathroom refresh or tile job — no permit needed. For a full gut remodel with moved plumbing or new GFCI outlets, we pull the permits for you. It adds a small cost but protects you at resale and ensures the work is inspected by the city.

How Long Does It Take?

  • Basic refresh: 3–5 business days
  • Tile and shower replacement: 5–8 business days
  • Full gut remodel: 2–4 weeks depending on complexity and permit timing

We don't work on multiple large projects simultaneously — when we're on your job, we're focused on your job. That's how we keep to the timeline we give you.

The Bottom Line

Don't get too hung up on national averages — those numbers include Bay Area and LA labor rates that have nothing to do with what you'll pay in Fresno. The best way to know what your project will cost is a free on-site estimate where I can actually see what you're working with.

I'll take measurements, talk through your goals, and give you an itemized quote — usually within 48 hours of the visit. No pressure, no commitment required.

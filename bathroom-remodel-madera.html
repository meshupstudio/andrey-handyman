Shower & Tub Replacement

Whether you're replacing a cracked tub with a walk-in shower or upgrading to a soaking tub, we handle the complete tear-out and rebuild. Options include:

  • Walk-in showers with custom tile or prefab surrounds
  • Frameless and semi-frameless glass enclosures
  • Freestanding soaking tubs and alcove tubs
  • Shower niches, benches, and accessibility features
  • Full waterproofing and membrane installation
Service

Tile & Flooring Installation

Tile work is where craftsmanship really shows, and it matters even more with the Central Valley's hard water — high mineral content that can leave calcium deposits on the wrong tile and grout. We help you choose materials that hold up, not just ones that look good on day one.

  • Floor tile in porcelain, ceramic, natural stone, and mosaic
  • Shower wall tile — subway, herringbone, vertical stacked, and more
  • Feature walls and decorative accents
  • Tile demo, substrate repair, and waterproofing
  • Grout sealing and caulking for a long-lasting finish

For a deeper look at tile and hard water, see our Central Valley tile guide.

Vanity & Fixture Updates

Updating your vanity is one of the highest-ROI moves in a bathroom remodel. We supply and install:

  • Single and double vanities — freestanding and wall-mounted
  • Undermount and vessel sinks with new faucet sets
  • Frameless and LED-lit mirrors
  • Vanity lighting, exhaust fans, and GFCI outlets
  • Toilet replacement and bidet installation
Service

Plumbing & Electrical

We handle plumbing and electrical in-house — no third-party coordination, no scheduling delays. Everything is done to California code and coordinated with the applicable local permitting requirements when a permit is needed.

  • Supply line and drain line installation or relocation
  • Water pressure optimization and shut-off valve replacement
  • Recessed lighting, vanity light bars, and dimmer switches
  • GFCI outlet installation (code required near water sources)
  • Exhaust fan installation and venting

Do I need a permit for a bathroom remodel in Madera?

Madera bathroom remodels follow the applicable City of Madera or Madera County building and permitting requirements.

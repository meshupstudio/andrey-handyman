Our Story

From Ukraine to the Central Valley — Built on Craftsmanship

Andrey Boginski's passion for construction started in childhood in Ukraine, where he spent countless hours taking apart and rebuilding everything he could get his hands on. That early curiosity grew into formal training in construction and remodeling, and eventually led him to California.

After settling in Fresno, Andrey founded what would become Andrey's Construction & Remodeling Inc. — a locally trusted name for bathroom remodels, kitchen renovations, and general home improvement across Fresno County.

What makes Andrey different is simple: he shows up. Every estimate, every project, every final walkthrough — Andrey is there. In 20+ years, he's built a loyal customer base not through advertising, but through word of mouth, one satisfied homeowner at a time.

Today, Andrey and his team have completed more than 500 projects across Fresno, Clovis, Sanger, and surrounding communities. And the standard hasn't changed: every home gets treated like his own.

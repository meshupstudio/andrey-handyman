Clovis Bathroom Remodel: Double Vanity & Patterned Shower

A completed bathroom remodel in Clovis, CA, pairing a double-sink vanity with a separate glass-enclosed shower finished in art-deco patterned tile.

LocationClovis, CA
Project TypeBathroom Remodel
Key FeaturesDouble Vanity & Tiled Shower

Project Overview

This bathroom remodel in Clovis, CA brought together two focal points: a double-sink vanity and a separate, glass-enclosed shower finished in a bold patterned tile. As with every project, Andrey personally oversaw the work from the initial measurements through the final walkthrough.

Design & Materials

The vanity area features a double-sink layout set into a granite-look, quartz-style countertop, paired with white shaker-style cabinetry and matte-black hardware and faucets. Just off the vanity, a separate shower is fully enclosed in glass and finished with white fan-shaped, art-deco patterned tile set against contrasting dark grout. The shower floor is finished in a black hexagon mosaic, and matte-black fixtures continue the hardware finish from the vanity. A small tiled corner shelf sits within the shower for everyday storage.

The Finished Result

The result is a bathroom that balances everyday function at the vanity with a standout patterned-tile shower as the visual centerpiece. It's a good example of what a double-vanity and shower remodel can look like in a Clovis home.
